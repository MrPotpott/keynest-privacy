Privacy Policy for KeyNest

Last updated: September 26, 2026

KeyNest is a local, offline password and information vault. This policy explains what data KeyNest does and does not handle.

What KeyNest does NOT do

  • KeyNest does not collect, transmit, or store any of your vault data on any server.
  • KeyNest does not require an account, email address, or sign-up of any kind.
  • KeyNest does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • KeyNest cannot see, access, or recover your vault contents. All data is encrypted on your device using a key derived from your master password, which is never stored or transmitted anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • All vault items (passwords, notes, Wi-Fi credentials, etc.) are stored locally on your device only, encrypted at rest.
  • Your master password is never stored in plain text and cannot be recovered by the developer if lost.

Optional actions you control

If you choose to export an encrypted backup, that file is created and shared only through your device's own share menu, to a destination you select (such as Google Drive or Dropbox). KeyNest does not upload this file automatically or retain a copy.

In-app purchases

KeyNest offers an optional one-time purchase ("KeyNest Pro") processed entirely through Google Play Billing. KeyNest does not receive or store any payment information; that is handled directly by Google.

Permissions used

  • Biometric/Fingerprint: Used only to unlock your vault locally on your device. No biometric data ever leaves your device or is seen by KeyNest.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
